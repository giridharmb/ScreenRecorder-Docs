Screen Recorder is preparing for publication. **No public installer or public app release is available yet.** The user guides and mock screenshots describe the current preview and may change before launch.

When the first public version is ready, this page will be updated with:

- The official download or purchase destination.
- The app version, release date, supported macOS versions, and Mac compatibility.
- Installation and update instructions for the chosen distribution channel.

There is no announced App Store listing, price, or release date. Documentation availability does not mean the app has been published or that a preview installer is ready for general distribution.
