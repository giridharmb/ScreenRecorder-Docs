# Set up audio

[← All guides](../README.md#user-guide)

## Choose the sound you want

| System / app audio | Microphone / audio input | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On | Off | Computer or application playback |
| Off | On | Your selected microphone or audio input |
| On | On | Application playback and microphone input |
| Off | Off | Silent video |

**System / app audio** captures playback directly. You do not need to point the microphone at the laptop speakers. In Window and Application modes, it follows the selected application; in Display and Area modes, it can include all system playback. The recorder excludes its own application audio.

**Microphone / audio input** records the chosen connected input. Choose **System default** or a specific device. Microphone access is requested when starting with that switch enabled. If a selected device disconnects, reconnect it or choose another input before starting.

## Listen at 10% while recording the original level

Enable **System / app audio**. The panel shows **Original source level · automatic**.

1. Keep the video player’s or source app’s own volume at full and unmuted.
2. Lower the **Mac’s speaker or headphone output volume** to a comfortable level — for example, 10%. You can also mute the Mac’s output.
3. Record as usual. The recording preserves the source audio level rather than reducing it with your listening volume.

This is automatic; there is no extra switch to enable or driver to install. The app does not turn up your speakers. Original level preserves the source; it does not make quiet material louder or normalize every recording to the same loudness.

Lowering the player’s own volume, muting a browser tab, or muting the source inside an application can lower or silence the recorded audio. Use the Mac’s output volume for quiet listening instead. Third-party mixers or unusual audio devices may alter the signal before it reaches the recorder, so make a short test with your setup.

This does not apply to a microphone listening to room sound. Lowering speaker volume makes that sound quieter in the room and can therefore reduce what the microphone hears.

## Browser audio and other tabs

Selecting one Firefox or Chrome window pins the picture to that window, but its sound can include other tabs or windows in the same application. This is not a per-tab audio selector. Pause or mute unwanted media in that app before starting.

For Display and Area recordings, check other apps too, because their playback can be included.

## Avoid echo and check the result

Use headphones when capturing both a microphone and application sound so the microphone does not also pick up the speakers. Choose the intended input and speak at a normal distance. Turn the microphone off if you only need clean application playback.

Make a short test, stop and save, then play the file with your Mac’s playback volume restored to a comfortable level. If sound is missing or doubled, see [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#audio-is-missing-quiet-or-doubled).

See [Recording Tools](recording-tools.md#check-your-sound) for the five-second playback check, live meters and gain/mute controls, M4A audio-only capture, and optional separate application/microphone files. Original-level capture remains the 100% default.
